# JULA launch gates

## Business and product approval

- Approve all proposed website copy, brand typography, visual use and public development references.
- Supply legal operator identity, address, registration/VAT details and verified customer contact channels.
- Approve each actual reference, ingredients, allergens/cross-contact, net quantity, servings, origin records, packaging, storage, shelf life and validated preparation instructions.
- Confirm exact discovery contents and quantities separately; currently displayed formats are proposals.
- Qualify selected suppliers; obtain evidence and permission for producer names, images, claims and certifications.
- Commission actual product and unbranded editorial photography; complete third-party image rights and attribution review.
- Complete reviewed French and Dutch content before enabling those editions.

## Before activating forms

- Review controller details, lawful basis, privacy text, hosting logs, processor contracts, retention, rights/contact process and security.
- Connect approved HTTPS receiving endpoints with the documented acceptance contract; test actual delivery and failures.
- Implement opt-in records, unsubscribe, suppression, deduplication and applicable double opt-in for newsletters.
- Use trusted proxy identity, shared abuse protection and distributed rate limits before public traffic.
- Rebuild after environment changes; validate both form labels and API behavior.

## Before commerce

- Select secure hosted checkout, authentication, inventory/order systems, tax handling, carriers, returns and customer support.
- Review consumer and professional terms, shipping, withdrawal/returns/refunds and payment processes for actual markets and products. No blanket food exemption is assumed.
- Add server-owned cart, checkout and product eligibility enforcement. The existing predicate is a content gate, not a implemented checkout or complete commerce validation.
- Validate batch traceability before activating Passport; no invented QR lookup, certificates or provenance records.
- Test accessible authentication, checkout, error recovery and transactional communications end to end.

## Deployment and review

- Configure the approved HTTPS domain, host authentication for private review, logs, backups and monitoring.
- Configure and test CSP/HSTS at the deployment boundary; never relax TLS to solve certificate problems.
- Run tests, typecheck, lint, build, dependency audit and browser tests against the production build.
- Complete manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast and mobile review beyond automated checks.
- Keep indexing disabled until business, content, imagery, legal and operational review is complete.
- Replace or approve working policy drafts before commercial publication. No production-readiness or accessibility certification is claimed by the current build.
